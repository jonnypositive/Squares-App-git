Football Squares by Jonny+ - Registrant Instructions

Welcome to the pool. This page explains exactly how to register, claim squares, and understand payouts and winner announcements.

How To Join

  • Use the Pool Registration section and enter your full name, email address, phone number, preferred payment app, and payment username/handle.
  • Once registered, your name appears in the registrant table.
  • Your email is used for winner announcements and pool messages.

How Squares Are Assigned

  • The administrator may assign squares manually or use Randomly Assign Registrants.
  • If random assignment is used, each registrant is placed into a square position automatically.
  • Square positions can be set before row/column digits are revealed.

How Winners Are Determined

  • At the end of each quarter, cumulative scores are entered in the Quarter Score Tracker.
  • The winning square is based on the last digit of each team score for that quarter.
  • The app highlights the winning square and displays the winner name in the Winning Square box.

How Winnings Are Calculated

  • Total registered funds are based on the active base square value and registrant count.
  • Each quarter has a payout percentage (default is 25% each quarter).
  • Quarter winnings are calculated as: Total Registered Funds x Quarter Payout %.
  • The administrator may adjust quarter payout percentages if needed.

Announcements

  • The administrator can send a quarter winner announcement to all registrants with one click.
  • Announcements include winner name, winning digits, and amount won.
